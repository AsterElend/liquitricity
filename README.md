# Liquitricity
Have you ever noticed how many tech mods have really robust fluid storage but not particularly good energy
storage without conversion to whatever their custom power unit is? No? 
Regardless, this mod allows you to convert electricity to fluid! It's called Crackle. \
As it uses the fabric fluid api and the tech reborn energy api, it should be compatibile with most
tanks and electricty sources across different mods.\
It also adds some small fluid storage items.\
Conversion is 1 energy -> 1 droplet. For those of you used to millibuckets, 1mb = 81 droplets.
### The Liquitricity Device
- Main feature of the mod
- Input energy on one side, and the device will attempt to store it in a fluid inventory on the opposite side
- Extract energy from one side, and the device will attempt to retrieve Crackle from a fluid inventory on the opposing side
- Works in any direction. Can even support multiple transfers on different axes simultaneously!

### The Fluid Chamber and Fluid Batteries
- Fluid Chamber can hold up to 4 fluid-storing items
- Pipes and other transfer systems will fill/empty the items in the chamber as if they were part of the chamber's own storage
- Fluid batteries come in 4 variants: Small (2 buckets), Medium (4 buckets), and Large (8 buckets)
- A fluid battery filled with Crackle will act as an energy-containing item, for systems that use those
- It can't be refilled directly with energy though

### Fluid Properties
- You can also build a cobblestone generator with the new Crackle fluid to produce prismarine. 
- Lava source blocks will convert to Sea Lanterns instead of Obsidian when using Crackle. 
- A basalt generator using Crackle will produce calcite as well.
